# Getting help

Nobody watches GitHub issues on this repository. These reach us:

- **Support page:** https://ado-analytics.baytekdev.com/support/
- **Email:** support@baytekdev.com

It helps to tell us your Azure DevOps organisation name, the version you are
on (Configuration, Help & Support in the extension shows it) and what you
expected to see.
Please leave work item titles and people's names out of screenshots where you
can.
