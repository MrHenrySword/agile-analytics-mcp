# Security

This covers Agile Analytics for Azure DevOps, its local AI connector (this
repository) and the Baytek service at https://ado-analytics.baytekdev.com.
It does not cover Azure DevOps itself, which is Microsoft's product and has
its own reporting channel.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please do not post security details in a public issue. Use one of these:

- Open a private security advisory:
  https://github.com/MrHenrySword/agile-analytics-mcp/security/advisories/new
- Or email support@baytekdev.com with the subject line `[SECURITY]`.

It helps to include what you found and what it could affect, the steps to
reproduce it, and the version you saw it in.

## What to expect

Baytek will acknowledge receipt within three business days. We follow a
90-day coordinated disclosure window from acknowledgement to public
disclosure, extendable by mutual agreement. We do not pursue good-faith
researchers operating within scope.

For confirmed security incidents that materially affect customer data, Baytek
will notify the affected customer's billing contact within 72 hours of
confirmation.
